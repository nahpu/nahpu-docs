## What NAHPU Provides

NAHPU is a free, account-free field catalog for natural-history collection and
biodiversity work. It can store records locally, capture or import media,
exchange selected records, display maps and statistics, and create exports,
backups, labels, and documents.

The App is a data-recording tool. It is not a permit, collection authorization,
institutional accession, chain-of-custody system, permanent repository, or
guaranteed archival service. Features may differ by platform, device,
permission, and connectivity.
