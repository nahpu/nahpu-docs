Dr. Austin S. Chipps is an evolutionary biologist at the National Museum of Natural
History, Smithsonian Institution, dedicated to collecting and using natural
history specimens to answer fundamental questions of mammalian evolution. The
research combines fieldwork with comparative genomics and phylogenomics to
understand the evolutionary mechanisms underlying the species richness and
morphological novelty of small mammals. Dr. Chipps at NAHPU works on quality assurance
and field-testing for NAHPU, and support the development of the mammalogy form.
