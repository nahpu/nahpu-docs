Dr. Arthur Porto is Assistant Curator of AI Biodiversity at the Florida Museum of
Natural History and leads the BioVision Lab, which develops computer vision and
machine-learning tools to understand the evolvability of complex organisms and
to extract high-dimensional phenotypic data from fossil and extant lineages.
Computer vision features and deep integration for genomics and phenomics are
currently in development for NAHPU in Porto's lab.
