Roberta Canton is a Brazilian evolutionary biologist working on how bird plumage
colors evolved, with a focus on structural colors studied through spectrometry,
electron microscopy, and molecular data. The research interests centre on animal
coloration, feather structure, and Amazonian biodiversity. A key contributor to
NAHPU development.
