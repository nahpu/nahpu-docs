Roberta Canton is an evolutionary biologist working on how bird plumage
colors evolved, with a focus on structural colors studied through spectrometry,
electron microscopy, and molecular data. The research interests centre on animal
coloration, feather structure, and Amazonian biodiversity.
Dr. Canton contributes to the development of the ornithology form and many aspects of
the user-interface design of NAHPU.
