Gregory Thom is Assistant Professor in the Department of Biological Sciences at
Louisiana State University and Curator of Genetic Resources at the LSU Museum of
Natural Science. The lab studies bird speciation in the tropics, investigating
the neutral and adaptive processes that shape genomes, population
differentiation, and cryptic diversity across the Neotropics, work that
regularly puts the team in a boat in the middle of the Amazon. A key
contributor to NAHPU development.
