Gregory Thom is Curator of Genetic Resources at the LSU Museum of
Natural Science and Assistant Professor in the Department of Biological Sciences at
Louisiana State University. The lab studies bird speciation in the tropics, investigating
the neutral and adaptive processes that shape genomes, population
differentiation, and cryptic diversity across the Neotropics, work that
regularly puts the team in a boat in the middle of the Amazon. Dr. Thom conceives
the design of the NAHPU tissue management system and oversees its implementation for genomic studies.
