Dr. Jacob A. Esselstyn is Curator of Mammals at the LSU Museum of Natural Science
and Professor in the Department of Biological Sciences at Louisiana State
University. The lab works on island biogeography, phylogenetics, systematics,
co-evolution, and community ecology, mostly with small mammals of Southeast Asia
and Africa, on the principle that you cannot preserve what you do not know
exists. The NAHPU user-interface layout and most of its core design were developed in the
Esselstyn Lab.
