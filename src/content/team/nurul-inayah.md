Nurul Inayah is a researcher at the Museum Zoologicum Bogoriense and the
Research Center for Biosystematics and Evolution of the National Research and
Innovation Agency, Indonesia, with research focused on small mammals,
marsupials, and monotremes. Inayah supports the development of the NAHPU mammalogy form,
the real-world field testing of the app in Indonesia, document translation, and
developing a NAHPU workshop program for the Indonesian mammalogy community.
