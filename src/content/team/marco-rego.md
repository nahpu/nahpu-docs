Dr. Marco Rego is a museum and field ornithologist at the Florida Museum of Natural
History, dedicated to improving the quality of museum specimens and their use in
research and outreach programs. The research relies on molecular data alongside
GIS tools and analyses to address questions of speciation, biogeography, and the
conservation of bird species and subspecies. Dr. Rego contributes GIS expertise
and support development of the ornithology form in NAHPU.
