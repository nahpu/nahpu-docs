Samantha L. Rutledge is a color scientist investigating avian color evolution,
especially how that evolution affects the heat load of birds. As an NSF graduate
fellow, the work covers the origin and function of avian iridescence and the
characterization of its structures and material properties, with an eye to
applications in material design and biomimicry. Contributed to the app
documentation.
