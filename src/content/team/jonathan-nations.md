Jonathan A. Nations is Assistant Curator of Mammalogy at the Florida Museum of
Natural History and a Research Associate at the Field Museum of Natural History,
working on evolutionary ecology, morphological evolution, macroevolution,
biogeography, and community ecology, with a focus on the mammals of the Malay
Archipelago. Central to the development of the NAHPU mammalogy form.
