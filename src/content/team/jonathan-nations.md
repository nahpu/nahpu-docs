Dr. Jonathan A. Nations is Curator of Mammalogy at the Florida Museum of
Natural History and Assistant Professor at the University of Florida. He is,
working on evolutionary ecology, morphological evolution, macroevolution,
biogeography, and community ecology, with a focus on the mammals of the Malay
Archipelago. Dr. Nations work is central to the development of the NAHPU mammalogy form
and the real-world field testing of the app.
