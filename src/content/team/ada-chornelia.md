Dr. Ada Chornelia is a postdoc at the School of BioSciences, University of
Melbourne, and a bat scientist working on the systematics, ecology, and
evolutionary history of bats. The research untangles the taxonomy of cryptic
species using multispecies coalescent methods together with integrated taxonomy
drawn from morphology, acoustics, and genetics, alongside historical
biogeography and stable hydrogen isotopes to track migration. Dr. Chornelia contributes to
the development of the bat acoustics form and writes NAHPU documentation and help message translation.
