Dr. Caleb Gordon is a postdoctoral researcher at the Florida Museum of Natural
History, studying the environmental and evolutionary processes behind
behavioral innovation in extinct mammals and reptiles. The work combines
paleontological methods with phylogenetic machine-learning algorithms and 3D
modeling, covering the evolution of aquatic and macrocarnivorous lifestyles in
reptiles, and novel AI workflows that assess how climate
change affected mammalian functional diversity.
Dr. Gordon conceives and oversees the design and implementaion of Paleontology support in NAHPU.
