Dr. Glaucia Del-Rio is Curator of Ornithology at the Florida Museum of Natural
History and Assistant Professor at the University of Florida. A Brazilian field
and museum ornithologist and evolutionary biologist, with work spanning
speciation, genomics, population genetics, and systematics through to spatial
ecology and conservation, and a particular passion for Amazonian birds.
Dr. Del-Rio works on the bird features along with developing
computer vision features for coloration studies in NAHPU.
