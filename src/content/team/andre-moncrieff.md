Dr. Andre E. Moncrieff is Curator of Ornithology at the Sam Noble Oklahoma Museum of
Natural History and Assistant Professor at University of Oklahoma.
His work investigates evolutionary patterns and
processes in birds with a focus on population genomics and biogeography. The lab
runs in-depth surveys of avian diversity in remote tropical regions, from Andean
ridges to Amazonia. Andre is second-in-command in NAHPU development and, with Heru
Handika, one of the two maintainers with full access to NAHPU codebase
management. Dr. Moncrieff conceives new app features, designs the bird taxon format, writes
documentation, tests the app, and expands collaboration with other museum curators.
