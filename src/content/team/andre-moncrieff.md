Andre E. Moncrieff leads a research lab at the Sam Noble Oklahoma Museum of
Natural History, University of Oklahoma, investigating evolutionary patterns and
processes in birds with a focus on population genomics and biogeography. The lab
runs in-depth surveys of avian diversity in remote tropical regions, from Andean
ridges to Amazonia. Second-in-command in NAHPU development and, with Heru
Handika, one of the two maintainers with full access to NAHPU codebase
management. Conceives new app features, designs the bird taxon format, writes
documentation, and tests the app.
