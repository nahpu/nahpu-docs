Dr. Natalia Pérez-Amaya is an ornithologist and Collection Manager of Birds at the Sam Noble Museum.
Her research interests are focused on the phylogeography and ecomorphology of
Neotropical birds. Dr. Pérez-Amaya contributes to bird catalog formats, app testing, and documentation and help message
translation.
